Report Tracker

See which of your gait reports are due, and by when. Setup takes about five minutes and you only do it once.

You have 7 business days from the date processing is completed to finish your report. Report Tracker reads your sessions from Moveshelf and shows a countdown for each one, so nothing slips past unnoticed.

It only ever reads. It never changes anything in Moveshelf.

Read this before you start. Do not run the app straight from a network folder. Copy the folder to your Desktop first, as described in Step 1.

Everything the app saves, including your Moveshelf key, goes into the folder the app is sitting in. If that folder is on a network drive, your key ends up somewhere your colleagues can open. The app refuses to run from a network folder for this reason, but it is easier to just start in the right place.

Work from your copy, not from the original folder. Everything the app saves, including your Moveshelf key, goes into the folder it is sitting in. On a network drive that would put your key where colleagues can open it, so the app simply refuses to run from there.

2Create your Moveshelf API key

An API key is how the app proves to Moveshelf that it is you. You create your own. It takes under a minute, and you only do it once.

Follow these exactly:

  1. Log in to Moveshelf in your web browser, as you normally would.
  2. Click the round profile symbol on the blue Moveshelf bar, just to the right of the word HELP and the little bell. See the picture below: there are two round buttons near the top right of your screen, and you need the lower one.
  3. A small menu drops down. Click SETTINGS.
  4. Check you are in the right place. The page should now be titled User settings, with your name and email on it. If you got a menu about your browser, favourites or passwords instead, you clicked the wrong button: go back and use the one on the blue bar.
  5. Scroll down that page until you see the API Keys box.
  6. Click in the box labelled Application ID* and type exactly: ReportTracker
  7. Click the blue Generate API Key button.
  8. Click Download Key. This is the step people miss. Nothing is saved until you click it.
  9. The file lands in your Downloads folder. Open Downloads, find the file that has just appeared, and move it into the Report Tracker folder on your Desktop, so it sits right next to MoveshelfReportTracker.exe.

Careful: there are two round buttons, one above the other. Your web browser has its own profile button in the very top corner of the window, and it sits almost directly above the Moveshelf one. Clicking it opens your browser's settings, not Moveshelf's.

How to move the file. Open your Downloads folder, click once on the key file, press Ctrl + X to cut it, then open the Report Tracker folder on your Desktop and press Ctrl + V to paste it in. You can also just drag it across if you have both windows open.

Three things people get wrong here.

  • Do not skip Download Key. Generating the key shows it on screen; downloading it is what actually saves the file the app needs.
  • The Application ID is not the key. ReportTracker is just a label you type in so you can recognise this key later. Do not copy it anywhere else.
  • Leave the file name alone. Whatever Moveshelf calls it, do not rename it. If the app cannot find it, rename it to mvshlf-api-key.json.

Your key is a password. It can read patient information. Do not email it, do not share it, and do not put it on a network drive. One key per person.

You can switch it off at any time: go back to the same Settings page, find ReportTracker in the list, and click the bin icon beside it. It stops working immediately and needs no IT ticket. Do this if you think anyone else has seen it, or when you change roles.

3Run it

The folder on your Desktop should now contain the key file alongside the app:

MoveshelfReportTracker.exe mvshlf-api-key.json Setup Guide.html READ ME FIRST.txt

Double-click MoveshelfReportTracker.exe. A black window opens, and your web browser follows a few seconds later. The first load takes about 15 seconds while it fetches your sessions.

Choose your site carefully. If your key gives you access to more than one, the app asks which one you work at.

Pick the Gait site for your location, for example shriners/CHI-Gait for Chicago or shriners/PHL-Gait for Philadelphia. The app preselects a Gait site for you, so usually you can just click Use this site.

Choosing a Sports site, or another location, gives you an empty or wrong list with no obvious reason why. If your list looks wrong, change the site using the dropdown at the top left, beside the app name.

It also asks which therapist you are, so it can show you your own work. You can change that later with the My sessions only tick box.

Where the list of names comes from. It is not a staff list. It is built from the therapist recorded on the sessions themselves, for your site and the date range you are looking at.

So if you have had no sessions in the last 90 days, your name will not be in the list. You can simply type it in, or widen the date range using the dropdown on the right.

If some sessions at your site have no therapist recorded, the list also offers (no therapist recorded) so those can still be found. They belong to nobody until someone fills the field in on Moveshelf.

Windows will probably warn you the first time. You may see "Windows protected your PC" or "unknown publisher". This is expected: the app is built in-house and is not signed by a commercial certificate authority.

Click More info, then Run anyway.

If there is no "Run anyway" option, your computer is configured to block unsigned programs and IT will need to approve it. Contact the person named at the bottom of this page.

To close it: click Quit in the browser page, or close the black window. To use it again another day, double-click the .exe again. Make a desktop shortcut if you want it one click away.

Do not bookmark the browser address. It changes every time you run the app, for security reasons. Always start from the .exe.

Using it day to day

The tiles across the top

Click any tile to show only those sessions. Hover over one to read what it means.

The line just above the tiles always says whose numbers they are. With My sessions only ticked they count just your work; otherwise they count every therapist at your site.

TileWhat it means
OverduePast the 7 business day deadline.
Due todayThe 7 business days run out today.
Due soonTwo business days or fewer left.
On trackThree or more business days left.
Awaiting processingData processing is not finished, so your clock has not started. Nothing for you to do yet.
No reportThis referral type does not produce a PT report, so no deadline applies.
BacklogMore than 30 business days past due. Usually finished work whose completion date was never entered.

Two things the app cannot know. Moveshelf records only when milestones are completed, never that a report is in progress. So "On track" means time remains, not that anyone has started writing.

And a report only counts as finished once its PT evaluation date is filled in. If you did the work but never entered the date, the app still shows it as outstanding.

Getting back to the full list

When you filter, an amber bar appears saying Showing a filtered list. Click Clear filters at the right of that bar, press Esc, or click the highlighted tile again. The Refresh button does not clear filters; it fetches newer data from Moveshelf.

Everything else

  • Click any row to open that session in Moveshelf. This is the quickest way to go and fix something.
  • Click the patient's name to open that patient instead, with all of their sessions rather than just this one. Handy when you need history from further back than the date range you are looking at.
  • The small button beside an MRN copies it, ready to paste into the EMR.
  • My sessions only narrows the list to your own work.
  • Search matches subject, MRN or therapist. Press / to jump straight to the box.
  • Sort by clicking any column heading.
  • Refresh re-fetches from Moveshelf. Press r.
  • Export to Excel, at the bottom of the list, saves exactly what is on screen. Filter first and you export only those rows.

How the deadline is counted

Business days are Monday to Friday, minus these nine holidays, which the app works out for every year on its own:

New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

A holiday falling at a weekend counts on the weekday it is observed: Saturday moves to the Friday before, Sunday to the Monday after.

If something goes wrong

Windows blocked the app.

See Step 3 above. If "Run anyway" is not offered, IT has blocked unsigned programs on your machine and will need to approve it.

It says to copy the app to my own computer first.

You are running it from a network folder. Copy the folder to your Desktop and run it from there. Nothing has been saved to the network folder.

Nothing happened when I double-clicked it.

Look at the black window for a message. If it closed instantly, the message scrolled past: contact the person below.

It says "No API key found".

The app tells you exactly which folder it looked in. Make sure your key file is in that same folder and is named mvshlf-api-key.json.

It says my key is not valid.

You almost certainly saved the Application ID instead of the key, or missed the Download Key button. Go back to Step 2 and follow it through to the end.

I clicked my profile picture and got browser settings, not Moveshelf.

You clicked your web browser's own profile button, in the grey bar at the very top of the window. The one you want is a little lower down, on the blue Moveshelf bar, to the right of HELP. See the picture in Step 2. You are in the right place when the page is titled User settings.

The list is empty, or shows patients I do not recognise.

You are probably on the wrong site. Use the dropdown at the top left, beside the app name, and choose the Gait site for your location.

My name is not in the therapist list.

The list is built from what is recorded on the sessions themselves. If your name is spelled more than one way in Moveshelf, open therapists.csv in your app folder: it lists every spelling found, and giving two rows the same canonical_name merges them.

A session I expected is missing.

Check the date range dropdown, which starts at the last 90 days. Then check whether its referral type has been switched off under Which referral types need a PT report?

It says the counts are out of date.

You left the tab open past midnight. It refreshes itself; the message is there so you never mistake yesterday's countdown for today's.

What the app does with your information

  • It only reads from Moveshelf and never writes anything back.
  • It runs entirely on your own computer. The page is served from your machine and cannot be reached from the network.
  • Patient data is never saved to disk. It exists only in memory while the app is running.
  • Export to Excel saves the rows you can see as a spreadsheet file. It contains patient names and MRNs, so keep it somewhere appropriate and delete it when you are done. Every export is recorded in the app's own log.
  • Your key is encrypted after the first successful run, so that only your Windows account on that computer can read it, and the plain copy is deleted.
  • A log records that data was fetched, with no patient details in it.
  • The page hides itself after a few minutes of inactivity, and the app shuts down on its own once you close the browser tab.
