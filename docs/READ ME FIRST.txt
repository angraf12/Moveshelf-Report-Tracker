REPORT TRACKER
==============

See which of your gait reports are due, and by when.


START HERE
----------

Open "Setup Guide.html" in this folder. Double-click it and it opens in your
web browser. It walks you through the whole thing in about five minutes.


THE ONE THING TO GET RIGHT
--------------------------

DO NOT run MoveshelfReportTracker.exe from a network folder.

Copy this WHOLE FOLDER to your Desktop first, and work from your copy.

Everything the app saves goes into whatever folder it is sitting in, and that
includes your personal Moveshelf key. If the app is on a network drive, your
key ends up there too, where other people can open it.

The app refuses to run from a network folder for that reason. Copying it first
saves you the detour.


IN SHORT
--------

  1. Copy this whole folder to your Desktop, and open your copy.

  2. In Moveshelf, click the round profile symbol on the BLUE MOVESHELF
     BAR, to the right of the word HELP. Then click SETTINGS.

     Careful: your web browser has its own round profile button in the
     very top corner of the window, just above it. That is the wrong
     one. The right one is lower down, on the blue bar that is part of
     the Moveshelf page. You are in the right place when the page is
     titled "User settings".

  3. Scroll down to API Keys. Type  ReportTracker  in the box labelled
     "Application ID*".

  4. Click "Generate API Key", then click "Download Key".
     (Do not skip Download Key. That is the step that saves the file.)

  5. Move the downloaded file out of your Downloads folder into the
     folder on your Desktop, next to MoveshelfReportTracker.exe.

  6. Double-click MoveshelfReportTracker.exe.

  7. If it asks which site, choose the Gait one for your location, such
     as CHI-Gait or PHL-Gait. It preselects a Gait site for you.

Windows will warn you about an unrecognised app the first time. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway". That is expected: the app is built in-house
and is not signed by a commercial certificate authority.
