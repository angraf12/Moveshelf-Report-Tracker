Beat 12 min

What it is

It shows a therapist which gait reports are due, and how many business days are left, counting from the date processing was completed.

  • Read-only. It never writes to Moveshelf. There is no code path that does.
  • Local. A small HTTP server bound to 127.0.0.1, rendered in the user’s own browser. No site, no server, nothing to deploy.
  • One person, one copy, one key. A personal tool, not a supervisor dashboard.
Say this out loud, early

It reports time remaining, never work remaining. Moveshelf records only completed-milestone dates — there is no draft, assigned or in-progress field anywhere. Checked across all 296 session metadata keys on 442 sessions.

So it can say a report is late. It can never say whether anyone has started it. A PT who hears otherwise will stop trusting the next thing it tells them.

Beat 25 mindemo

The demo, in order

Drive the real app. Do not narrate the code.

  1. Land on the worklist. “Every session at my site. The tiles across the top are the counts.”
  2. Click the Overdue tile. “Tiles are filters. Click one and you get that pile, nothing else.”
  3. Turn on My sessions only. “This is what a PT actually works from.”
  4. Sort by Days Left. Point at a negative number. “Negative is past due, and the row is red.”
  5. Click a row. “Opens that session in Moveshelf. Click the patient name instead and you get the subject.”
  6. Point at the Foot Model column and the tinted rows. “Foot model processing waits on x-ray measurements, so a late row of that kind means something different.”
  7. Hover a tile for its tooltip. “Every bucket explains itself. Nobody has to memorise these.”
  8. Export to Excel. “It exports the rows you can currently see, and nothing else.”
Correction to the 28 July email

That email said there was “deliberately no export.” True when written, wrong now. CSV export was approved for clinical use on 30 July, with three controls that stay: visible rows only; every export written to logs/access.jsonl (row count and filename, never the rows); and values starting = + - @ prefixed so Excel cannot execute them as formulas.

Correct it before someone else does. Read-only against Moveshelf is still true and always will be.

Beat 34 min

The clock, which is the whole product

Seven business days, starting the day processing was completed. Weekends and nine holidays are skipped. Everything else in the app is presentation.

  • One field stops the clock: the PT evaluation date in the EMR. Fill it in and the row goes to Done, however late it was.
  • Nine holidays, computed rather than typed. New Year’s, MLK, Memorial, Independence, Labor, Thanksgiving and the Friday after, Christmas Eve and Christmas. Nothing needs updating each year, and a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before.
  • Good Friday is not one of them. That is a decision, not an oversight. If your site observes it, say so and it goes into holidays.txt as a local adjustment rather than into the code.
Beat 43 min

The buckets, and the one that gets misread

BucketWhat it means
⚠Overdue Past the 7 business day deadline that started when processing completed.
⏰Due today The 7 business days run out today.
••Due soon Two business days or fewer left.
✓On track Three or more business days left. Time remains — not that the report has been started. Moveshelf does not record that.
⋯Awaiting processing Not a therapist’s backlog. Data processing is unfinished, so the clock has not started and nothing is owed yet. Waiting on whoever processes the data.
–No report This referral type does not produce a PT report, so no deadline applies.
▪Backlog More than 30 business days past due. Usually finished work whose completion date was never entered.
“Awaiting processing” is the one people get wrong. The label was changed away from “Not started” for exactly that reason. An engineer who repeats the old wording to a PT hands them an accusation.

Backlog exists because at CHI-Gait a third of processing-complete sessions had no EMR date at a median of 111 business days past processing — reports finished and never dated. The count is never hidden and the days-left number is never altered; only the bucket changes.

Beat 52 min

Why some rows owe nothing

Many referral types never produce a report. The app carries a list of those, and the shape of the evidence is worth a minute, because you will be asked to adjust it for your own site.

The rule that must not be flipped

It is an exclusion list, not an inclusion list. An unrecognised referral type still requires a report. The app fails toward showing work, never toward hiding it. So when your site invents a new referral type, it appears on the worklist until someone decides otherwise — which is the safe direction to be wrong in.

Beat 65 mininstall

Installing it, and the one rule that matters

  1. Copy the whole ReportTracker folder to the Desktop and work from the copy. Never run it from a network drive.
  2. Open Setup Guide.html from the copy. It covers all of this with pictures, and it is what you hand anyone who missed this session.
  3. Generate a personal key in Moveshelf: the profile symbol on the blue Moveshelf bar, right of HELP → SETTINGS → API Keys → Application ID ReportTracker → Generate API Key → Download Key. The browser has its own profile button just above Moveshelf’s. You are in the right place when the page is titled “User settings.”
  4. Move the downloaded key file out of Downloads and into the folder, beside the exe. The most common failure is pasting the Application ID instead of the key.
  5. Run the exe. Windows warns about an unrecognised app: More info, then Run anyway. Expected. Built in-house, not signed by a commercial certificate authority.
  6. First run asks for the site (your location’s Gait project) and which therapist you are, and remembers both. About five minutes, once per person.

A refresh of roughly a hundred sessions takes 15–18 seconds. That is the Moveshelf call, not the app, and the page shows a loading state. Warn people, so it does not read as a hang.

Beat 73 min

What it touches

Someone in the room will be asked this by their manager within a week. Give them the answer in a form they can repeat.

  • The key is personal, like a password. Never emailed, never shared, never on a network drive, one per person. Revocable instantly from the same Settings page with no IT ticket. After the first successful connection it is stored DPAPI-encrypted, readable only by that Windows account on that machine.
  • Access is bounded by the person’s own key and by nothing else. That is the only boundary here that means anything.
  • “My sessions only” is a display convenience, not a security control. The whole site’s data is already in the page. Never describe it as restricting what someone can see — that is a promise the app does not keep.
  • It does display PHI — patient names and MRNs — to the therapist responsible for those patients. That is the point of the tool, and it is why the key rule and the desktop rule are not negotiable.
Beat 83 min

What your PTs will ask you

Open these live if the questions come up. Otherwise leave them for people to read afterwards.

Windows says it is not safe. Should I stop?

No. More info, then Run anyway. It is built in-house and not signed by a commercial certificate authority, which is exactly what SmartScreen is reporting. If Windows blocks it outright rather than warning, that is a different thing — tell me.

It says “Awaiting processing” but I already wrote that report.

That bucket means data processing is unfinished, so the seven-day clock has not started. It is not a comment on the therapist. The row is waiting on whoever processes the data.

It is red, but I finished that report weeks ago.

The app can only see dates. The PT evaluation date in the EMR is what stops the clock, so an empty field keeps the row open however finished the work is. That is also why the Backlog bucket exists.

My name is not in the therapist list.

Names are normalised, because one person is spelled several ways in the live data. If yours still does not appear, use Other name… and type it. Sessions where Moveshelf records no therapist at all collect under (no therapist recorded) — at some sites that is a large share, and without that entry they would belong to nobody.

Can I just run it from a network drive?

No, and it will refuse to start. Everything it saves lands beside the exe, including your key, so on a network drive your key would sit where colleagues can open it.

I lost my key, or I have a new laptop.

Generate a new one and revoke the old one, both from the Moveshelf Settings page. Instant, no ticket, nothing to reinstall.

Why does it take so long to refresh?

15–18 seconds for about a hundred sessions, and it is the Moveshelf call rather than the app. Known, and worth revisiting only if it actually gets in people’s way — so say so if it does.

Does it change anything in Moveshelf?

No. There is no write path in it at all.

What I need back from you

This is a prototype, and the parts I cannot test from here are exactly the parts you are standing in front of.

Does it run on your managed machines? The SmartScreen wording, antivirus quarantine, anything that blocks it outright. This is the single biggest unknown, and the main reason for this session.
Do your therapist names come through? People missing or duplicated, and how big your (no therapist recorded) pile turns out to be.
Which referral types at your site owe no report? The seeded list is CHI-Gait’s. Yours will differ.
Do you observe holidays we do not compute? Good Friday is the likely one.
Anything confusing, anything wrong, anything missing. Prototype feedback is worth far more now than after a wider release.
Close on the ask rather than a summary: “Install it on one real machine this week, and tell me what Windows did.”
